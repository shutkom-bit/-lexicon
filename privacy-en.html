Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 28 August 2026

This page explains what data Lexicon collects, why, and with whom it may be shared. By using the app, you agree to what is described below.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Lexicon is an independent educational project created by the legal entity TOV «TRACKAUTO» (LLC; registration code EDRPOU 38872343), 30 Biloruska St., Kyiv 04050, Ukraine. Contact for privacy matters: office@trackauto.com.ua. You can send any request regarding your data to this address (see section 7).

2. What data we collect

  • Your Google account (via Google sign-in): your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Words and phrases you add, together with their translation, transcription, examples and topic.
  • Learning progress: card state (spaced repetition), quiz and game results, day streak, records.
  • Technical session data in your browser (local storage): which set is active, display settings. These are not advertising cookies and are not sent to us.
  • Telegram link (only if you connect the bot yourself): the chat identifier used to deliver your words into the app.

We do not collect payment data (payments are not yet enabled) and we do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information.

3. Why we process data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service itself — to store your dictionary and progress (performance of a contract / our legitimate interest).
  • To generate translations and examples via AI at your request (based on your action).
  • To keep the service running, secure, and to prevent abuse.

4. Who data is shared with (service providers)

We do not sell your data. To run the app we use third-party services:

  • Google / Firebase — sign-in and data storage. The database is hosted on servers in the European Union (region europe-west3).
  • DeepSeek — AI enrichment of words. When you add a word, its text is sent to DeepSeek for processing. This provider is located outside the EU/EEA (China), so this involves an international transfer of data. For this reason, do not enter any sensitive personal information as "words" (names, passwords, medical or financial data, etc.).
  • Vercel — app hosting.
  • Telegram — only if you connect the bot: exchanging messages with the bot.

5. How long data is kept

Your dictionary and profile are kept for as long as you use the app. You can delete individual words at any time or request deletion of your whole account (section 7). After account deletion, the data associated with you is removed from our database.

6. Shared sets (teacher feature)

If you share a set by code, the words in that set become available to anyone who has the code — they receive a copy of the set in their own dictionary. Do not share content you do not have the rights to. If you involve students, you are responsible for having a proper legal basis for doing so (see section 8 on children).

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to: access your data, correct it, delete it (the "right to be forgotten"), obtain a copy (export), withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. To exercise any right, write to office@trackauto.com.ua. A dedicated "Delete account" button will be added to the app; until then, deletion is carried out on request to this address.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you are under this age, use Lexicon only with an adult's consent. Teachers who involve underage students are responsible for ensuring appropriate parental consent under local law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page; we will mark material changes with a new date at the top.
